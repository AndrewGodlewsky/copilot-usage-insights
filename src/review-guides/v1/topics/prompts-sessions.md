Prompt length excludes attachments/injected context and cannot measure completeness. Bounded excerpts test goals, constraints, or acceptance checks; they are not representative.

Depth counts confirmed human submissions in selected dates, not lifetime depth. Steering/queued messages count without separate charges. Uncertain identities and absent metadata are excluded. Missing logs understate depth/prior activity; no prior record does not prove a new conversation. Multi-day use is not continuous work or one task.

Prompt-level calls require complete, finished, reliably linked work (including continuations/subagents across dates); the joint chart needs every session prompt accounted for. Calls reflect autonomy, iteration, retries, or verification, not task duration, count, difficulty, or success. Do not rate session length or recommend arbitrary resets.

Delivery is timing (idle, steering, queued); unknown stays unknown. Session steering rates require all deliveries known. Concentration reflects recorded steering; unknowns can hide more. Repetition may reflect correction, new info, scope changes, or collaboration, not poor prompting. One or two cases cannot establish a habit; do not infer a negative assessment from frequency alone.

Compare equal calendar periods with sufficient samples and coverage within 10 percentage points; workload/logs may differ and change is not improvement. Never invent unexported timelines.

Stops are explicit local-user aborts, not prompts or necessarily caused by a message. Other aborts are excluded; missing metadata does not prove no stops. Never label associated spending wasted or infer causation from timing; state alternatives and coverage.
