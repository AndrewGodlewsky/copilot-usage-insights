Prompt length excludes attachments/injected context and cannot measure completeness. Bounded excerpts can test goals, constraints or acceptance checks; they are not representative.

Depth counts confirmed human submissions in selected dates, not lifetime depth. Steering/queued messages count even without separate charges. Uncertain identities and absent metadata are excluded. Missing logs understate depth/prior activity; no prior record does not prove a new conversation. Multi-day use is not continuous work or one task.

Prompt-level calls require complete, finished, reliably linked work, including continuations/subagents across dates. The joint chart needs every session prompt accounted for: samples differ. Calls can reflect autonomy, iteration, retries or verification, not task duration, count, difficulty or success. Do not rate short/long sessions or recommend arbitrary resets.

Delivery is timing: idle starts work, steering joins work, queued waits. Unknown stays unknown. Session steering rates require every delivery known. Concentration describes recorded steering; unknowns can hide more. Repetition may mean correction, new information, changed scope or collaboration, not poor prompting. One or two cases cannot establish a habit; do not infer a negative assessment from frequency alone.

Compare equal calendar periods with sufficient samples and coverage within ten percentage points. Tasks/logs may differ; change is not improvement. Never invent unexported local timelines.

Stops are explicit local-user aborts, not prompts or necessarily caused by a message. Other aborts are excluded; missing metadata does not prove no stops. Never label associated spending wasted or infer causation from timing. State alternatives and coverage.
